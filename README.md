
# AI Dignity Lab

AI Dignity Lab is a free, plain-language checker that tells everyday people whether an AI-written job posting, customer reply, parent letter, or lesson talks down to someone, leaves someone out, or runs into the law, and shows exactly why, with a cited source and a better way to say it.

Built by Victory Ajayi as a ChiEAC Fellow with the Chicago Education Advocacy Cooperative.

**Status:** In development (Nov–Dec 2026)
